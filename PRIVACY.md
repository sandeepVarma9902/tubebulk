# Privacy Policy — TubeBulk

**Last updated:** October 4, 2026

TubeBulk ("the extension") does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer or any third party, except as strictly required for its core functionality described below.

## YouTube data
- The extension accesses your YouTube channel's video list (titles, descriptions, privacy status) and updates video descriptions **only when you explicitly click Apply**.
- Access uses Google OAuth; the access token is held in the browser's local session by Chrome and is never sent to the developer.
- No video data, tokens, or account information are transmitted to any server operated by the developer.

## Payments
- Purchases are processed by **ExtensionPay** (extensionpay.com). The developer does not see or handle your payment details.

## Local storage
- The extension stores only: your free-tier usage count and your payment status, using Chrome's `chrome.storage` APIs on your device.
